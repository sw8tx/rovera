# Platform and item-transfer review — required before listing

Rovera buys digital goods from users and sells its own inventory to customers. Users do not sell to one another. No platform below is represented as approving Rovera or any particular sale. Keep listings disabled until the specific product, region, source account, and delivery method have passed a documented review.

For every proposed listing, record the platform's current terms and applicable regional rules, the rights attached to the item, and a permitted transfer path. Do not request passwords, session cookies, recovery codes, or access to a seller's personal account. Test fulfillment with an authorized method, define buyer remedies and support, and obtain legal/compliance review where required. Recheck after relevant platform policy changes.
